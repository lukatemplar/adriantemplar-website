Behind the music

Music humanly played.

Adrian Templar is a songwriter, composer and arranger working across indie pop, electro-pop and rock.

Adrian's visual identity is brought to life through an AI-generated avatar, while the music itself remains deeply human — written, composed, arranged and performed by a real musician.

His songs bring together organic instruments, modern production and cinematic storytelling — always with a human heart.
