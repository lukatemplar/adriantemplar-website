About Adrian

Adrian Templar is a songwriter, composer and singer whose music blends indie pop, electro-pop and rock influences. His songs explore movement, change, resilience and the moments that bring us closer to ourselves.

Each release unfolds through music and cinematic storytelling, connecting the sound to a wider visual world.
